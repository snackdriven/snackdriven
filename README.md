### snackdriven · Senior QA Engineer

**I find bugs for money and introduce them for fun.**

I practice DDD: *dopamine-driven development*. It explains the repo count.

Mostly I build the tools I wished existed. Sometimes that means replacing my keyboard's software. Sometimes it means keeping track of horror movies or making impossible decisions about Disney movies. Both ship.

For my personal builds, AI writes the code; I own the specs, workflow design, testing, and quality bar.

### Work I keep coming back to

- [AL80 Studio](https://github.com/snackdriven/al80-studio): reverse-engineering for spite, resulting in browser controls for my keyboard's screen, RGB, and keymap, tested on the actual hardware.
- [Now Screaming](https://now-screaming.snackdriven.com/): horror releases and where to watch them, for the movie I meant to see three weeks ago and have already forgotten.
- [Felt Presence](https://felt-presence.snackdriven.com/): a haunted craft shop that sends letters, with illustrated stories, wandering greenery, and an automated publishing workflow.
- [Personal QA Workflow](https://snackdriven.com/portfolio/projects/qa-workflow.html): bringing the ticket, the RFC, and that decision in Slack together before testing, then backing findings with recordings and network checks.
